Sudoku

Fill every row, column and box with 1 to 9. A fresh daily puzzle every day.

New game

How to play
  • Tap a square, then tap a number. Every row, column and 3×3 box must have 1 to 9 exactly once.
  • Notes lets you pencil in small numbers you are not sure about.
  • A wrong number turns red and counts as a mistake. Three mistakes ends the game.
  • You get 3 hints per game. Your game is saved, so you can come back any time.
