About Ambaro

Ambaro is a home for games people in India grew up playing, and new ones made here. Play against the computer, pass one phone around, or open a room and play with friends.

Our games

  • EKAH: our own card game, built around the four elements Agni, Jal, Prithvi and Vayu.
  • Ludo and Snakes & Ladders: the classics, with rooms for friends.
  • Chess and Sudoku: with lessons for beginners.

Our promise

Ambaro is free to play. There is no real money: no betting, no deposits, no cash prizes. Everything you win stays in the game.

Who runs it

Ambaro is an independent project, made and run by an individual developer in India.
