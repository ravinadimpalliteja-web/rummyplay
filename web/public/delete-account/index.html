Delete your account

Royal Club Games · Arugu app

You can ask us to delete your Royal Club Games account at any time. This applies to accounts used on the website and in the Arugu Android app.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to info@royalclubgames.com with the subject "Delete my account".
  2. Include your username. If you added a recovery email, send the request from that address so we can confirm the account is yours. If you did not add one, we may ask you to confirm by signing in.
  3. We will confirm and delete your account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • your username and display name;
  • your sign-in details (password hash) and recovery email, if you added one;
  • any game data linked to your account on our servers.

What is kept

  • Our hosting providers may keep technical security logs, such as IP addresses, for a short period before they are deleted automatically.
  • Purchase records kept by Google Play are handled by Google under its own policies.

Data on your phone

Settings and stats from games against the computer are stored only on your device. To remove them, uninstall the app or clear its storage in your phone's settings.

See our Privacy Policy for more details.
