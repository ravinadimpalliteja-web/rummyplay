ROYAL CLUB GAMES

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy explains what information Royal Club Games collects when you use our games, the Royal Club website at royalclubgames.com and our Android app (together, "the service"), why we collect it, and the choices you have. Royal Club Games is run by Nadimpalli Ravi Teja, an individual developer in India. You can reach us at info@royalclubgames.com.

In short: our games are free to play and involve no real money. You can play against the computer without an account. If you create an account, we keep your username and, only if you add one, a recovery email. We do not sell your information.

Information we collect

If you play without an account (against the computer, or with friends on one phone), your game settings, progress and simple stats such as games played and won are stored only on your own device. We do not receive them.

If you create an account (needed to create or join online rooms), we collect:

  • your username and display name, which other players in your rooms can see;
  • your password, which is stored only in hashed form by our sign-in provider; we cannot read it;
  • a recovery email address, only if you choose to add one. We use it to let you reset your password and to contact you about your account. It is never shown to other players.

When you play online rooms, our room server processes the room code, the usernames of the players in the room and the moves of the game, so that everyone sees the same game. This information is kept in memory while the room is active and is not used for anything else.

Technical information. Like any website or app that connects to the internet, our hosting providers automatically process technical data such as your IP address, device and browser type, and the time of requests. This is used to deliver the service, keep it secure and fix problems.

We do not collect your contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera data.

How we use information

  • to run the games, accounts and online rooms;
  • to let you sign in and recover your account;
  • to keep the service secure and prevent abuse or cheating;
  • to fix bugs and improve the games;
  • to answer messages you send us.

Advertising and purchases

We plan to show ads and offer optional in-app purchases, such as removing ads or cosmetic themes. When these are added:

  • ads in the Android app will be served by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID and similar information to show and measure ads. You can reset or limit the advertising ID in your Android settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services;
  • in-app purchases in the Android app are processed by Google Play. We receive confirmation of what you bought, but not your card or bank details.

We will update this policy before these features go live.

Services that process information for us

  • Supabase: player accounts and sign-in;
  • Render: the online room server;
  • Cloudflare: hosting the website and game files;
  • Google: app distribution through Google Play, and later ads (AdMob) and purchases (Google Play billing).

These providers may store and process information on servers outside India. We only share what each one needs to provide its part of the service. We do not sell or rent your personal information.

How long we keep information

  • Account information is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your username, display name and recovery email within 30 days.
  • Online room data is kept only while the room is active.
  • Information on your device stays until you clear the app's storage, uninstall the app, or clear your browser data.
  • Hosting providers may keep technical logs for a short period for security.

Your choices and rights

  • Play without an account: games against the computer work without signing up.
  • Delete your account: see how to delete your account.
  • Access or correct your information: email us and we will help.
  • Withdraw consent: you can remove your recovery email or delete your account at any time.

You may also have rights under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and other laws that apply to you. Contact us to use them.

Children

The service is intended for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use secure connections (HTTPS) and trusted providers, and passwords are stored only in hashed form. No online service can be completely secure, so please use a password you do not use anywhere else.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page, and for important changes we will tell you in the app or on the website.
